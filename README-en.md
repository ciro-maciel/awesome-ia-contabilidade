## Privacy in AI

Where to turn off the use of your conversations to train the model.

- [Anthropic: onde mudar o treino](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-anthropic-onde-mudar-o-treino) - How to stop your conversations with Claude from being used to train the model. `official`
- [Google: atividade do Gemini](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-google-atividade-do-gemini) - How to turn off Gemini's activity history and what changes with it. `official`
- [Microsoft: controles de privacidade do Copilot](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-microsoft-controles-de-privacidad) - Where to find the privacy and training controls for Copilot. `official`
- [OpenAI: controles de dados](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-openai-controles-de-dados) - How to stop conversations on ChatGPT from being used to train the model. `official`
- [WhatsApp: privacidade](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-whatsapp-privacidade) - What WhatsApp says about privacy, including when you talk to Meta AI. `official`

## Professional Standards

What the CFC publishes and what applies to those who keep client data.

- [CFC e COAF](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-cfc-e-coaf) - The CFC page on communications to COAF. `official` `free`
- [NBC PG 01, o Código de Ética](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-nbc-pg-01-o-codigo-de-etica) - The Professional Code of Ethics for Accountants. `official` `free`
- [Resolução CFC 1.640/2021](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-resolucao-cfc-1-640-2021) - CFC rules on preventing money laundering for accountants. `official` `free`

## Official Sources to Check

Deadlines, tables and obligations, straight from those who set them.

- [Agenda Tributária](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-agenda-tributaria) - The month's deadlines, published by Receita Federal. `official` `free`
- [Certificação digital no ITI](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-certificacao-digital-no-iti) - ITI's answers about digital certificates. `official` `free`
- [Lei Complementar 123/2006](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-lei-complementar-123-2006) - The Microenterprise Statute and Simples Nacional. `official` `free`
- [Lei Complementar 214/2025](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-lei-complementar-214-2025) - The consumption tax reform law: IBS, CBS and Imposto Seletivo. `official` `free`
- [Manual de Orientação do eSocial](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-manual-de-orientacao-do-esocial) - The eSocial manual for payroll and labor events. `official` `free`
- [MEI: perguntas frequentes](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-mei-perguntas-frequentes) - Official answers about the individual microentrepreneur (MEI). `official` `free`
- [Procuração eletrônica no e-CAC](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-procuracao-eletronica-no-e-cac) - How the client grants power of attorney to the firm without handing over the digital certificate. `official` `free`
- [Tabelas do Imposto de Renda 2026](https://go.mymarketing.click/iacont-tabelas-do-imposto-de-renda-2026) - The year's IRPF income tax tables, at Receita Federal. `official` `free`

## Tags

Each item says what it is. The tags next to it help you choose: official when it comes from whoever sets the standard, open source when the code is published, free or paid based on the usage model, API and SDK based on how you integrate.
